## Support plans at a glance

| Feature / Plan | Free Plan | Premium Plan |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| **Monthly price** | 0 USD | 9 USD |
| **Contact channels** | Email | Video call or email |
| **Support priority** | General queue | Absolute priority case handling |
| **Work sessions** | Not included | 1 free session per month (max. 15 minutes) |
| **Response time** | Up to 7 calendar days | Within 24 hours on business days |

## Free plan

Included with every ChronoTally installation at no cost:

- **Email support** in the general queue.
- **Response time** of up to 7 calendar days.
- **Platform maintenance**: preventive and corrective work that keeps ChronoTally stable, available, and free of errors.

## Premium plan

An optional plan at **9 USD per month**. It includes everything in the Free plan, plus:

- **Absolute priority case handling** - Premium tickets are handled ahead of the general queue.
- **Video call or email** - request a call when email back-and-forth is not enough.
- **One free work session per month** (max. 15 minutes) with the team.
- **24-hour response time on business days.**

### Premium response and resolution

- The response target is **24 hours on business days**.
- A "response" means the technical acknowledgment of receipt, the initial problem diagnosis, and severity assignment. It does not necessarily mean the immediate final resolution of the incident.
- If the response time is missed more than **three (3) consecutive times in the same billed month**, the Licensee receives a credit equal to **10% of the monthly fee** on the next invoice. This credit is the sole compensation available for missed response times.

### Premium work sessions

- One session of up to **15 minutes per month** is included at no extra cost.
- Additional sessions are available at **6 USD per 15-minute block**.

## Scope of support

Support covers **platform maintenance**: preventive and corrective tasks performed by the maintainers to ensure stability, operational availability, and error correction of the application's base system.

The following are **not included** and are quoted separately as implementation work:

- Custom development or code changes.
- Customizations and specific configurations.
- Third-party integrations.
- Data migration.
- Design or business-logic changes.

## Contacting support

- **Email:** support requests on both plans are submitted by email. The address is configured in ChronoTally's own `chronotally/config/support.py` (or overridden per site with the `chronotally_support_email` key in `site_config.json` / `common_site_config.json`) and is published on the in-app Support page at `/chronotally/support`.
- **Video call (Premium):** request a call in your support email and the team will schedule it.

## Business days

Business days are Monday through Friday, excluding official holidays and mandatory rest days in the USA.
